Robosuite settings and protocol

Panda, OSC_POSITION, 20 Hz; robosuite 1.5.1 and MuJoCo 3.2.3. Actions: three position increments and one gripper command. Observations: flat 68D; stage-aware 75D. APPROACH fixes the gripper open in both interaction and optimization.

Both backbones: hidden layers [256,256], learning rate 3×10⁻⁴, γ = 0.99, τ = 0.005, batch 256, replay 300000, random exploration 5000 steps, updates after 1000. TD3: actor delay 2, exploration noise SD 0.1, target noise SD 0.2, clipping 0.5. SAC: automatic temperature, initial 0.2, temperature learning rate 3×10⁻⁴.

Seeds 0–4. Single-policy budget: 2M steps and 1000-step episodes. Stage timeout: 300 steps. FSM budget: 1M per stage, advancing at 80 successes in the latest 100 complete episodes or budget exhaustion. Handoff pools hold 256 states. Success needs three consecutive confirmations and at least five stage steps.

Checkpoint selection: 50 original-reset episodes, seeds 200000–200049, at episode boundaries after 50k intervals and at training completion. FSM validation starts after the first complete PLACE episode. Maximize complete-task success and keep the earliest tie. Final testing: 100 original-reset episodes, seeds 100000–100099. TD3 uses no exploration noise; SAC uses the squashed Gaussian mean.

APPROACH: horizontal error <0.03 m and end effector 0–0.05 m above object center. GRASP: bilateral fingerpad contacts. LIFT: retained grasp and center-height gain ≥0.08 m. MOVE: retained grasp, XY error <0.035 m, Z error <0.03 m, speed <0.15 m/s; center target is bin floor + half-height +0.05 m. PLACE: official success, release, speed ≤0.05 m/s.

MG400 deployment conditions

The independently trained PPO stages are APPROACH and PUSH. Their simulation progression criterion is 90 successes in the latest 100 complete episodes, distinct from PickPlaceCan’s 80% threshold. PUSH uses successful APPROACH terminal simulator states. PPO uses its own distance-based rewards, not the potentials above.

APPROACH: 0.02 m tolerance, 0.07 m rear-Y offset and reference Z −0.123 m. Per-axis object-displacement tolerance is 0.001 m in simulation and 0.01 m in deployment. PUSH target square: 0.10 m side. Simulation confirmation and minimum dwell are one step; physical switching uses one feedback sample.

Vision uses red/blue calibration anchors for independent axis scales and a blue-anchor offset with swapped image/robot axes. It is not depth or homography reconstruction. Object Z is configured at −143 mm; no plane-height/object-center calibration record or independent mapping-error measurements are available. During APPROACH the initial object observation is held; PUSH refreshes detections with last-position fallback.

Deployment interprets robot-frame coordinates as training-frame positions after millimeter-to-meter conversion. Policies output absolute XYZ setpoints; a 3 mm measured-tip-anchored waypoint is converted to millimeters for MovL. The robot waits one second and reads pose feedback after commands; no fixed physical feedback frequency is established.

One successful physical execution is reported. Multiple runs occurred without a complete trial log, so no aggregate physical success rate is estimated.
